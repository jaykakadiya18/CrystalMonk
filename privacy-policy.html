Cookies & Tracking Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and track your activity on our website.

Types of Cookies We Use:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Cookies To maintain login and shopping cart during your visit Until browser closes
Persistent Cookies To remember preferences and login information Months or years
Analytics Cookies To track website usage and improve functionality Months
Marketing Cookies To show personalized ads and track campaigns Months or years

Managing Cookies:

  • You can control cookie settings in your browser preferences
  • Disabling cookies may affect website functionality
  • Most browsers have "Do Not Track" (DNT) options
  • You can opt-out of marketing cookies at any time

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA grants you additional rights:

  • Right to Know: What personal information is collected and how it's used
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information (with exceptions)
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We won't discriminate for exercising your CCPA rights

To submit a CCPA request, email: ccpa@pureastrology.com

GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to your data:

  • We process your data based on your consent or legitimate interests
  • You have enhanced rights to access, correct, and delete your data
  • You can request data portability in a machine-readable format
  • You have the right to lodge complaints with your local data protection authority
