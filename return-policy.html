Exchange Policy

If you'd like to exchange your stone or bracelet for a different variety, size, or style, we offer free exchanges within 30 days of purchase.

Exchange Process:

  1. Contact our support team with your order number and reason for exchange
  2. Receive an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number
  3. Ship the original item in its original condition
  4. Upon inspection and approval, we ship your new item
  5. If the new item costs more, you pay the difference
  6. If the new item costs less, credit is issued as store credit

Types of Exchanges Available:

  • Different Stone Type: Exchange Ruby for Sapphire, Diamond for Emerald, etc.
  • Different Weight: Upgrade to a heavier stone or downgrade as needed
  • Different Quality: Exchange lower quality for premium astrological graded stones
  • Size Variations: Exchange bracelet sizes if the fit isn't perfect
  • Metal Changes: Swap between silver, gold, or copper settings

Defective Products

If you receive a defective, damaged, or misrepresented product, we'll replace it immediately at no cost, regardless of the return window.

What Qualifies as Defective:

  • Cracks, chips, or fractures in the stone that were not visible before opening
  • Broken clasps, threads, or settings on bracelets
  • Stones that don't match the authenticity certificate or grade
  • Manufacturing defects in metalwork or stringing
  • Products significantly different from the product description

How to Report Defects:

  1. Contact us within 7 days of receiving the product
  2. Provide clear photos or videos of the defect
  3. Include the authenticity certificate and order number
  4. We'll arrange immediate replacement or refund
